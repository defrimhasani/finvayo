# Product Delivery Phases

## Phase 1: Complete Cash Entries

- Edit payments and expenses.
- Mark planned entries paid with actual amount and date.
- Include or exclude entries from the forecast.
- Configure monthly recurrence.
- Treat past-due expected income as overdue automatically.

## Phase 2: Weekly Review and Follow-Up

- Guide users through cash confirmation and entry review.
- Record completed weekly reviews and show the last review date.
- Generate editable invoice follow-up messages in three tones.
- Copy messages and record completed follow-ups.

## Phase 3: Deeper Planning

- Support fixed and percentage tax reserves.
- Store a typical customer payment-delay assumption.
- Explain forecast contributors and limiting events.
- Run server-calculated purchase scenarios and convert them into expenses.

## Phase 4: Data Controls

- Export workspace data in JSON and CSV.
- Reset the financial plan while preserving the account.
- Delete the workspace and owner account with explicit confirmation.
